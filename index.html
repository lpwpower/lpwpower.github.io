About

GEN

I am a Ph.D. student at Yale CS, co-advised by Professors Rex Ying and David van Dijk in the Graph & Geometric Learning Lab and van Dijk Lab.

My research interests lie at the intersection of LLM agents, multi-agent systems, and efficient LLM adaptation and inference (MoE, LoRA, KV cache), together with memory management and retrieval-augmented generation. Previously I worked on AutoML, OOD generalization, causal discovery, and graph learning, with applications in biochemistry and biomedicine.

Before Yale, I received my Master's degree from Tsinghua University, advised by Prof. Wenwu Zhu, and was fortunate to intern with Menghua Wu in the Regina Barzilay Group at MIT CSAIL, and also at Alibaba Cloud under the Alibaba Innovative Research Program.
